# AR sourcing: first pass

**The ask:** find an analyst relations lead who already has relationships with the Gartner analysts covering enterprise AI coding agents. Tiered list, draft note, yes/no.

**What I found:** there is no publicly findable AR owner at Cursor, Anthropic or OpenAI. I checked their sites, newsrooms, job boards, press releases, event speaker lists and 17 AR agencies. AR at those companies sits inside comms or an agency, and agencies don't name AI lab clients. So the tier 1 search is a LinkedIn and referral problem, not a scrape. Public data gets you the names below and tells you where to look next.

**Top 3 on public evidence**
1. Ryan Ragozzine, Staff AR Manager, GitLab. Ran the 2024 and 2025 MQ cycles with these exact analysts. GitLab just dropped out of the 2026 quadrant. Best match, most reachable.
2. Stephanie Look, Head of AR, Google. IIAR AR Professional of the Year 2025. Strongest resume, hardest to pull.
3. Rachel Carbino, GitHub. Only name at a current Leader. Evidence is self reported, verify before reaching out.
